
Use the following Regex to extract all the alphanumeric characters after the word DRYVAN
EXAMPLES 
DRYVAN-356
DRYVAN L2
DRYVAN 54-689

It will extract 356, L2, 54689

(?i)DRYVAN[-\s]*([A-Za-z\d\-]+)

(?i) makes it case-insensitive.
DRYVAN[-\s]* matches "DRYVAN" followed by zero or more spaces or hyphens.
([A-Za-z\d\-]+) captures one or more alphanumeric characters or hyphens.
